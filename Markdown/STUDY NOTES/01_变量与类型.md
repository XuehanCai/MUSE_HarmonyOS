## 标题
  变量与类型
## 场景
all
## 现象
生成变量
## 正确写法

```ts
let title:string ='Creative orange vase'
console.log('The price of the product is',title)

let count:number = 1
console.log('The count of the product is',count)

let isSelect:boolean = true
console.log('Has the order been selected')
```

## 记忆点
let 名 ： 类型 = 值
